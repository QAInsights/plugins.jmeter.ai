# Blog Featured Image Generation Rules

When asked to generate a blog featured image (e.g., using `generate_image` tool):

- **No Text**: Do NOT include any words, letters, text overlays, titles, or logos in the image.
- **No Humans**: Do NOT include human figures, faces, or bodies.
- **Glassmorphism Render**: High-premium render featuring translucent glassmorphic layers, frosted
  depth, light refractions, and sleek floating visual elements.
- **Color Palette**: Use electric lime or green, shades of purple and blue, with subtle accents of
  yellow.
- **Abstract & Geometric Shapes**: Use modern abstract or geometric shapes, 3D curves, and clean
  technological forms.
- **Top-Tier Quality**: The render must look ultra-professional with Apple- or Google-level
  aesthetic precision, feeling completely unique and state-of-the-art.
